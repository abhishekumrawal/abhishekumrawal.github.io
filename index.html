Research

Research

Optimization, learning, and economic models for decision-making in digital platforms, networks, and AI systems.

Digital Platforms & Algorithmic Decision-Making

How should platforms allocate resources, target users, and design incentives when behavior propagates through networks?

I study influence dynamics, peer effects, targeting, incentive design, and sequential decision-making in networked and platform environments. My work develops optimization- and learning-based methods for resource allocation and behavior-aware decisions when actions interact through social or information networks.

This agenda connects computational models of diffusion with substantive questions in digital markets: how firms should distribute incentives, how reinforcement changes word-of-mouth, how platforms should allocate scarce resources, and how adaptive decision policies perform when feedback is limited or uncertain.

Representative directions

  • Fractional marketing incentives and diffusion under budget constraints
  • Social reinforcement, word-of-mouth, and network targeting
  • Attention regulation and strategic learning on digital platforms
  • Sequential learning and influencer selection under limited feedback

AI Systems & Strategic Behavior

How can AI systems remain controllable and trustworthy when models and users interact strategically?

I study the safety, control, and strategic behavior of generative AI systems. I develop causal and game-theoretic approaches to controllable text generation, manipulation and intent-hiding, and interactions between AI systems and users.

A central theme is causality: identifying mechanisms inside modern AI systems, intervening on latent representations, and understanding how strategic agents adapt to defenses. This work connects trustworthy AI with optimization, game theory, and sequential decision-making.

Representative directions

  • Game-theoretic attack and defense in large language models
  • Causal steering of text generation through latent representations
  • Causal analysis of mixture-of-experts models
  • Strategic behavior, manipulation, and AI governance
Methods: optimization, online learning, machine learning, causal inference, game theory, stochastic modeling, and network science.
